Privacy policy

Stock Analyst · last updated 2026-10-19

Stock Analyst is a personal research tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It has one user, its owner, and is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • Gmail, read-only (the gmail.readonly scope), for the owner's own Google account only.
  • It searches only for messages from a single sender of trade alerts.

How it uses that data

  • It reads those alerts to price and analyze the trade they describe.
  • Derived trade details (ticker, strike, expiry and prices), never the email text, are sent to an AI model (Anthropic Claude) to write an analysis for the owner.

What it stores, and where

  • Parsed alerts and analyses are stored on the owner's computer only.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, used for advertising, or sent anywhere else.
  • It never sends, changes or deletes email.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
